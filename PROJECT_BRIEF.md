## Lomda-wide rules (גורף)
- **Arrows:** at the end of a video the forward arrow blinks; a click on it moves on, "לאורך כל הלומדה". The back arrow returns to the previous screen, throughout (slides 8, 9).
- **Check button:** every question has "צדקתי?" (the storyboard's wording, not "האם צדקתי?").
- **Tries:** a first wrong answer shows "זו אינה התשובה, נסו שוב. תוכלו ללחוץ על כפתור העזרה כדי לקבל רמז."; a second wrong answer or a correct one opens the feedback of the next slide (slide 10 note).
- **Help button ("עזרה"):** opens and closes a hint on every question, "לאורך כל השאלות בלומדה" (slide 15 note). The hint text is the speech bubble at the top of each question slide.
- **Scoring:** equal weight for every question; a question with parts splits its weight equally between the parts (slide 42 note). 70 and above is reported "בוצע בהצלחה", with the score (see open-questions.md).
- **Numbers are from 2025** (credit line on slide 3).
- **Accessibility of the graph:** numbers and months must be accessible in size, readability and alt text (slides 31, 33 design notes).
